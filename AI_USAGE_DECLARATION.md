Declaration of Generative AI Use - IMTPS

Generative AI tools, including Claude and ChatGPT, were used during the development of IMTPS as supporting tools.

They were mainly used for discussing requirements, reviewing code and architecture, debugging support, UI design suggestions, and assisting with documentation.

AI suggestions were reviewed and tested before being incorporated into the project. The team remained responsible for the project's implementation, design decisions, testing, and final submitted work.
